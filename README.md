## About Me

I am a **Business Administration student, Business Analyst, Client Research and Outreach professional, and technology builder** based in Ottawa, Ontario. My work brings together business analysis, prospect research, stakeholder communication, project coordination, data-driven decision-making, responsive web development, and product thinking.

I am the **Co-Founder of ENVEI**, a workforce-measurement platform focused on understanding how skills, education, and labour-market needs align. Through ENVEI, I have researched prospective government and post-secondary clients, built and maintained a client pipeline, gathered requirements, explained product capabilities to stakeholders, coordinated with technology vendors, and helped translate a complex workforce problem into a practical product direction.

I also build and study responsive web experiences, analytics products, automation workflows, and digital strategies. My public GitHub work includes HTML projects, GitHub Pages, layout and flex components, accessibility coursework, Vue, Astro, a contact-book project, and a static-site project. My live portfolio expands this work into SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Google Analytics, Zapier, Microsoft Power Automate, CRM systems, SEO, content marketing, social media marketing, email campaigns, and conversion optimization. My approach is to keep learning while building work that is useful, understandable, accessible, and connected to real user and business needs. Explore the complete portfolio at [bimbola-coker-pro.lovable.app](https://bimbola-coker-pro.lovable.app/).

I am open to **business analysis, business development, client research, product operations, project coordination, web development, UX research, technology partnerships, internships, and mission-aligned collaboration**.
